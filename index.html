VB

I'm a public policy researcher and program consultant based in Virginia. I'm currently pursuing a Master of Public Administration at James Madison University, after starting my career at the intersection of international business, sustainability, and wildlife conservation across Rwanda, Cameroon, Côte d'Ivoire, and the UK. I love turning messy data and stakeholder input into recommendations people can actually use. Interested in working together or having a chat? Feel free to contact me.

Researching

Across policy, sustainability, and wildlife-economy projects, I've designed surveys, analyzed data in R, SPSS and NVivo, and turned quantitative and qualitative findings into research outputs people can act on.

Managing

As an Odoo consultant and staff manager, I've built MVPs tailored to clients' workflows, tracked projects across teams, and kept multiple research and consulting engagements moving on schedule.

Communicating

I enjoy teaching, public speaking, and writing — from leading a fundraising campaign for wildlife conservation to co-authoring papers on ethics in public and nonprofit governance.

State of the Wildlife Economy Reports

As a research intern with ALU's School of Wildlife Conservation, I collected data on ecotourism, carbon markets, hunting, and non-timber forest products, contributing case studies to the State of the Wildlife Economy reports for Africa and Benin.

Cross River Gorilla Fundraising Campaign

I led a campaign for the African Conservation Foundation that raised $25,000 to protect cross-river gorillas in southwest Cameroon, directing a 20-person team and coordinating roughly 1,000 awareness articles.

UN & World Bank Youth Engagement

I joined the UNA-NCA cohort at the Global Engagement Summit at UN Headquarters, and was selected as a Youth Delegate for the 2026 World Bank Youth Summit, where our team placed among the Top 6 Innovation Lab finalists.

Policy Research at JMU

As a Graduate Research Teaching Assistant, I support Introduction to Public Policy instruction and contribute to 4+ active faculty research projects, from Qualtrics survey design to preliminary data analysis.

Odoo Consulting & Enablement

As Senior Consultant at AD Finance, I designed client-tailored MVPs, ran webinars and conferences, and wrote guides and tutorials that helped onboard new clients internationally. Certified Odoo V16.

Team & Project Management

Arboretum Program & Web Overhaul

At the Edith J. Carrier Arboretum, I designed a student employment program framework, directed an 80-guest donor appreciation event, and helped overhaul the arboretum's website via JMU's Cascade CMS.

Leadership & Community
